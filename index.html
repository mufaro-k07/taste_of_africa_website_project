Latest food trends

Old grains, new tables.

Cooks across the continent and in the diaspora are turning back to millet, sorghum and fonio, grains that grew here long before supermarkets arrived. They are naturally hardy, full of character and easy to love once you taste them.

Street food steps up.

From grilled skewers sizzling at the roadside to fried dough sold warm from a bucket, street food has become the most honest ambassador of African flavour. Plenty of home cooks now borrow the same flavours for their own kitchens.

The comeback of the fermented.

Soured porridges, fermented milks and pungent seasoning pastes were once everyday kitchen wisdom. Today they are being rediscovered for their depth of flavour and the way they stretch a harvest.
