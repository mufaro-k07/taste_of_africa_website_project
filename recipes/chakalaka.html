Chakalaka

Southern Africa

The story

Chakalaka is the kind of dish that belongs to everyone and no one. Many tell the story that it began in the townships around Johannesburg, where cooks stretched tomatoes, peppers, carrots and a tin of baked beans into a spicy relish for hungry workers.

Today it is a fixture at the braai and a must beside warm bread or sadza. Cooks all have their own heat level, so start gentle and add chilli until it makes you smile.

Method

Prepare
  1. Chop and grate all the vegetables so they are ready to go.
  2. This dish cooks quickly.
Cook
  1. Soften the onion in oil, then add garlic, ginger and curry powder and cook for a minute.
  2. Add the peppers, carrots and chilli, then the tomatoes.
  3. Cook until the vegetables are tender but still bright, then stir in the baked beans.
Serve
  1. Simmer for 10 minutes until glossy.
  2. Serve warm or cold with sadza, bread or grilled meat.
Serves
4 as a side
Time
About 35 minutes
Difficulty
Easy

Ingredients

Quantities shown for 4 servings.

  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 thumb of ginger, grated
  • 2 carrots, grated
  • 1 green pepper, diced
  • 1 red pepper, diced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tin baked beans, about 400 g
  • 1 tablespoon curry powder
  • 1 fresh chilli, chopped, to taste
  • Salt to taste
