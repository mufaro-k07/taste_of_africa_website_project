West Africa

West Africa

West Africa cooks with fire, pepper and patience. Pots simmer for hours, markets smell of smoked fish and fresh chillies, and every dish arrives with a story and a debate.

Food culture

Meals are social and generous. Family compounds share big bowls, street vendors turn grills into stages, and celebrations are measured by how much jollof is on the table.

Staples

Rice, yam, cassava, plantain, millet and groundnuts, cooked with palm oil, onions, tomatoes and scotch bonnet peppers.

Cooking techniques

Slow simmered stews, one pot rice dishes, pounding yam into smooth, stretchy dough, and open fire grilling of spiced skewers.
