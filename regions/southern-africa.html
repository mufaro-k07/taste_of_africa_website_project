Southern Africa

Southern Africa

Southern Africa gathers around fire and a pot of maize. It is a region of braais, shared plates and relishes that make plain food sing.

Food culture

The table is communal. People eat with their hands, share from one plate, and cook outdoors whenever the weather allows. Relishes of greens, meat and vegetables turn simple maize into a feast.

Staples

Maize, sorghum, beans, groundnuts, leafy greens, beef and dried fish.

Cooking techniques

Cooking over open fire, slow stews in cast iron pots, stirring thick maize porridge, and drying meat and fish to keep for later.
