East Africa

East Africa

East Africa is where the Indian Ocean meets the highlands. On the Swahili coast, every meal carries the scent of spices that arrived by sea.

Food culture

Hospitality is serious here. Tea is poured, plates are piled high, and sharing is expected. Coastal cooking blends African, Arab and Indian influences into something entirely its own.

Staples

Rice, maize, cassava, beans, coconut, bananas and fish, flavoured with cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and cumin.

Cooking techniques

Simmering in coconut milk, layering spices into rice, charcoal grilling and quick fresh salads like kachumbari.
